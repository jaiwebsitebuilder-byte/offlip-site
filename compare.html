What to look for

  • The tracker should be free. Counting days and money is the whole point. If an app charges for that after three days, the reviews will tell you how people feel about it.
  • A slip shouldn't wipe you out. Most people need more than one try. An app that sends you back to day zero is measuring the wrong thing.
  • A cut-down option. Cold turkey isn't the only way to start. A plan that steps you down to a quit day, with the money still counting, is easier to begin.
  • Your data on your phone. Quitting is private. An app that needs an account has your quit history on a server somewhere.
  • A glanceable counter. A widget or lock-screen counter means you can see progress without opening an app full of reminders of the thing you're quitting. Offlip's is coming with the store release.
