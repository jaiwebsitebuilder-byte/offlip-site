Privacy Policy

Your quit data stays on your phone.

Effective October 17, 2026 · Last updated October 17, 2026

The short version

  • No account. No ads. No tracking. No selling or sharing of your data.
  • Your plan, streak, cravings and slips are stored only on your device. We never receive them.
  • If you join our waitlist on this website, we keep your email to tell you when Offlip launches.
  • Apple handles payments. We never see your card details.

This policy explains how Jai Pannu ("Offlip", "we", "us") handles information when you use the Offlip mobile app (the "App") and the website at jaiwebsitebuilder-byte.github.io/offlip-site (the "Site"). If you have questions, email jaiwebsitebuilder@gmail.com.

1. Information stored on your device (we don't receive it)

To work, the App saves the following only on your device, in the App's local storage:

  • Your plan: what you used (pouches, vapes or cigarettes), how much per day, what it cost, your quit date, and the reasons and triggers you chose.
  • Your activity: cravings you logged and how they went, slips and their size, and the highest rank you've seen.

This information does not leave your device unless you choose to move it, as described below. We have no server that receives it, and we can't see, access, recover or delete it for you.

Your choices: you can change your plan in Settings, export a backup file, restore from a backup, or delete all of it with "Delete all my data" in Settings. Deleting the App also deletes this data. If you lose your phone without a backup, we can't restore your progress.

2. Information you choose to share

  • Backups. "Export a backup" creates a file and opens your phone's share sheet. Wherever you send or save that file (for example Files, iCloud Drive, email or a messaging app) is your choice, and that service's own privacy policy applies. The file contains the information listed in section 1.
  • Share cards. When you share a receipt or milestone card, it's an image you send through your phone's share sheet. It can show your money kept, days, counts and rank. Nothing is posted unless you send it.
  • Links to support services. The App links to free services such as the EX Program, SmokefreeTXT, Smokefree.gov and 988. Opening them takes you to those services, and their privacy policies apply.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through your Apple ID. We never receive your name, card or billing address. To check whether you have Premium and to restore purchases, the App uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase receipts and basic device details (such as app version and country), and gives us aggregate purchase reports. It does not receive your quit data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

4. Crash reports from Apple

If you've turned on "Share with App Developers" in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash and performance reports. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

5. Our website and waitlist

  • Waitlist. If you join the waitlist, we collect your email address and your confirmation that you're 18 or older. We use them only to tell you about Offlip's launch and major updates. Our email provider, Brevo (Sendinblue SAS), stores them for us. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your email at any time.
  • Fonts and hosting. The Site loads fonts from Google Fonts, and our hosting provider keeps standard server logs. Both receive your IP address and browser details as part of loading the page.
  • The Site's cost calculator runs in your browser. The numbers you enter are not sent to us.
  • The Site uses no advertising or tracking cookies.

6. How we use information

We use the limited information we receive (your waitlist email, purchase status and Apple crash reports) to send launch emails you asked for, to provide and restore Premium, to fix bugs, and to meet legal obligations. We don't use it for advertising, we don't build profiles, and we don't sell or share personal information, as those terms are defined under U.S. state privacy laws.

7. Health information

Information about your nicotine use and quitting is sensitive. In the App it stays on your device, as described in section 1. Joining our waitlist may suggest an interest in quitting nicotine. We treat your waitlist email as consumer health data: we collect it only with your consent, use it only to tell you about Offlip, never sell it, and delete it on request. Residents of Washington, Nevada and other states with consumer health data laws can use the contact below to exercise their rights, including access, deletion and withdrawing consent.

8. Keeping and deleting information

On-device data stays until you delete it or the App. We keep waitlist emails until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, or until 24 months after launch, whichever comes first. Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat for as long as they need them for billing and tax.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to get a copy, to correct it, to delete it, and to appeal if we refuse. Email jaiwebsitebuilder@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days, or within the time your local law requires. We won't treat you differently for using these rights. Because your quit data never reaches us, requests about it are best handled by using "Delete all my data" in the App.

10. Age limit

Offlip is for adults 18 and older. The App asks for your birth year at the start. If you're under 18, it does not let you continue, points you to free help made for teens, and saves nothing. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you think a minor has joined our waitlist, email us and we'll delete it.

11. Security

On-device data is protected by your phone's own security, such as your passcode and Face ID, and by iOS app sandboxing. Backup files you export are not encrypted by Offlip, so store them somewhere you trust. No method is perfectly secure, but because we don't hold your quit data, there's nothing of it on our side to breach.

12. Where data is processed

We're based in the United States. Our service providers (Brevo, RevenueCat, Google and our hosting provider) may process the limited information described above in the United States, the European Union or other countries, under their own safeguards.

13. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we handle information we've already collected, we'll tell you in the App or by email before it takes effect.
